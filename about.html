About Kaspar

Engineering-focused vacuum and compressed-air equipment manufacturer serving overseas industrial customers.

Kaspar Intelligent Equipment (Dongguan) Co., Ltd. is a subsidiary of Guangdong Zhongde Pump, strategically established to tap into the overseas market. The company operates a professional team with over 20 years of industry experience, a production base covering 20,000 square meters, and has mastered more than 40 patented technologies. Its products have passed ISO 9001 and CE certification, and it provides customized intelligent equipment solutions for global customers.

  • Professional engineering team with deep vacuum technology background.
  • Production base of 20,000 m² with in-house machining, assembly and testing.
  • More than 40 patented technologies across pump design and structure.
  • Customized intelligent equipment solutions tailored to each application.

Who We Serve

Customers across Asia, Europe, the Middle East and beyond, from single-pump supply to integrated vacuum stations and after-sales support.
