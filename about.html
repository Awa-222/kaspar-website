About Kaspar

Kaspar focuses on the design, manufacture and supply of vacuum and compressed-air equipment for industrial customers. The team supports overseas buyers from product selection and configuration to delivery and after-sales service.

🏭

Manufacturing Focus

Vacuum pumps, integrated pump skids, root blowers and screw compressor packages, with components selected for stable long-term operation.

🧩

Application Engineering

Equipment is configured around each customer's suction capacity, pressure, voltage and site conditions, rather than one-size-fits-all supply.

🌍

Overseas Support

We serve customers across Asia, Europe, the Middle East and other markets, providing spare parts, wearing kits and responsive technical follow-up.

How We Work

A straightforward process from enquiry to after-sales.

1. Requirements

Share your application, required capacity, pressure and power supply.

2. Selection

We recommend the pump or package configuration and confirm specifications.

3. Production & Inspection

Units are assembled and tested before packing and dispatch.

4. Delivery & After-Sales

Spare parts, maintenance guidance and technical support after handover.

Quality Commitment

Tested Before Dispatch

Each unit runs through performance and leakage checks before leaving the workshop.

Traceable Components

Motors, seals, filters and wearing parts are sourced to matched specifications and documented in the supply record.

Responsive Communication

Quotations, technical answers and order updates are followed up promptly through email and chat channels.
